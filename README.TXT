CAT SIMULATOR 64
by illobo/netmilk

Two cats in an 80s living room: a black tabby with white socks (yours)
and a grey one with a mind of its own. There is a ball of yarn, a couch,
a food bowl and a computer on the desk. Next door is the piano room,
with its own grey cat, a sofa, a plant and a blue ball of yarn.

STARTING
  LOAD "CATSIM64",8,1 and RUN (or open catsim64.prg on an Ultimate).
  On the title screen press fire on a joystick in port 2, or a button
  of a 1351 mouse in port 1. Both work in the game whichever you used.

CONTROLS
  Move        mouse in port 1, or joystick in port 2
  Play        left mouse button, or fire:
                grab the ball, toss it when you carry it
                steal the ball from the grey cat (it hisses)
                boop the grey cat (it meows back, or hisses if
                  it is asleep or eating)
                eat at the bowl, or paw it when it is empty
                hop onto the couch, the sofa or the piano
                scratch the plant or the sofa's arm
                meow, if nothing is in reach
  Leap        push up and press the button:
                from the couch to the desk (mind the lamp)
                over the plant, or over the sofa's arm
  Next room   on the desk, keep going right.
              In the piano room, go left through the door.
              F1 switches room from anywhere.
  Nap         right mouse button, or hold fire for a second
  Wake up     move, or click
  SPACE and 1 work as the left and right buttons too.

REQUIREMENTS
  A C64 (PAL) or C64 Ultimate / Ultimate 64, a 1351 mouse or a joystick.
